Cadence

Manage audio on USB storage from your browser.

Is this your Wasmo?

This app only opens for its owner. Sign in to your Wasmo, then open Cadence from your launcher.

Once it's open you can install it to your desktop: use your browser's Install app.

Install Cadence on your own Wasmo

  1. In your Wasmo, open the computer menu and choose Install App.
  2. Paste this address: https://github.com/yschimke/cadence/releases/latest/download/cadence.wasmo
  3. Name it cadence and open it. Settings, staged audio and chosen folders stay in the browser you use it from.

USB discovery and writing to the device's folder need desktop Chrome or Edge.

Other ways to use Cadence

  1. In your browser, with nothing to install.
  2. On Android and the desktop: releases.
